# LectureIQ
AI-powered study assistant that transforms lectures, PDFs, videos, and text into simple explanations, summaries, quizzes, important topics, and expected question papers.
Students can upload a youtube link or an MP4 video of a lecture and the website offers the following features:
Structured Study Guide: Generates a concise 3-paragraph summary and 5 key takeaways.
Active-Recall Quiz: Creates a 5-question multiple-choice quiz with instant feedback and explanations.

Context-Aware Q&A Chat: Ask questions grounded strictly in the lecture transcript (prevents AI hallucinations).

Syllabus & PYQ Analyzer: Ingests syllabus and past-year questions (text) to rank important topics and predict the exam paper structure.

The Game-Changer: Exam Readiness Map: After taking the quiz, the tool maps wrong answers back to the high-priority syllabus topics to generate a personalized "Study This Next" action plan. (This is what NotebookLM doesn't do).

Demo Safety Net: A pre-processed "Load Demo" button so your live pitch never fails.

Users can upload pyqs and website will give expected question paper 

Finally there is a 2d revision game where the student has to choose the right door among 3 doors which is the right answer to the asked question about the video lecture uploaded.
